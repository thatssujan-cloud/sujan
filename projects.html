Northwind Analytics Dashboard

Product Design & Front-End Development · 2024–2025

Challenge

The operations team tracked performance across 40+ spreadsheets, making trends nearly impossible to spot until it was too late to act on them.

Approach

I ran three rounds of user interviews to map the decisions the team actually needed to make, then designed a single dashboard around those decisions instead of the raw data.

Outcome

The team now catches performance issues days earlier on average, and onboarding new hires to the reporting workflow dropped from two weeks to two days.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • D3.js
  • Node.js

Fernbank Design System

Design Systems & Front-End Development · 2023–2024

Challenge

Four product teams were shipping visually inconsistent UI and re-solving the same interaction patterns, which slowed everyone down.

Approach

I audited existing patterns across all four products, consolidated them into a token-based system in Figma, then built the matching component library in React with full Storybook documentation.

Outcome

New features now ship roughly a third faster, and design QA time dropped significantly once teams had a shared, tested set of components.

  • Figma
  • React
  • Storybook
  • Design Tokens

Trailhead Trip Planner

Product Design & Front-End Development · 2021–2022

Challenge

Users bounced between four separate apps to plan a single trip, and most trips never made it past the planning stage.

Approach

I designed one guided flow combining search, itinerary building, and budget tracking, then built it as a responsive web app so it worked well on mobile without a native app.

Outcome

Trip completion — users who finished planning and booked — increased meaningfully in the months after launch, and mobile session length nearly doubled.

  • React
  • Node.js
  • Mapbox
  • Responsive Design
